Hello! I'm a systems engineer working across networking, embedded systems, robotics, and edge AI, from infrastructure and device communications to custom hardware and intelligent automation.

My background is in large-scale network and systems engineering, but most of my current work lives where software, electronics, AI, and physical systems meet.

-- What I'm Working On --

* **Agentic AI & Robotics:** Developing application stacks, middleware, distributed control systems, and agentic AI integrations for robotics and automation at GSN Robotics. This includes integrating local AI models with physical systems, sensors, telemetry, and embedded controllers.

* **Embedded & Physical Systems:** Designing custom multilayer PCBs, integrating microcontrollers and sensors, developing device communications, and building prototype hardware using KiCad, FreeCAD, Blender, and 3D printing.

* **Edge AI:** Fine-tuning and evaluating Hugging Face models using LoRA/QLoRA, quantization, and local deployment techniques for resource-constrained and edge-computing environments.

* **Agent Orchestration:** Building a framework that separates higher-level AI reasoning from deterministic control paths, allowing agents to coordinate with software services, embedded devices, automation systems, and low-latency reflex endpoints.

-- Tech & Infrastructure Toolkit --

* **AI & Robotics:** Python, Node.js, ROS 2, Gazebo Sim, RViz, Hugging Face Transformers, LoRA/QLoRA, Quantization.

* **Hardware/EDA:** RP2350, ESP32, sensor integration, telemetry, KiCad, Gerbv, FreeCAD, Blender, 3D Printing.

Before moving deeper into embedded systems, AI, and robotics, I spent over a decade designing, securing, and operating enterprise and service-provider infrastructure. My career has taken me from supporting region-wide ISP networks with hundreds of routers and subnets, to industrial PLC-connected environments, GE Research & Development at NASA Michoud Assembly Facility, and systems administration supporting operations at NASA Kennedy Space Center.
